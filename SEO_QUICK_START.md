## 💡 How It Works

### The Strategy
1. **Multiple Pages, One Event**: Each page targets a different search keyword
2. **Keyword-Rich Content**: Every page includes the keyword naturally
3. **Internal Linking**: Pages link to each other (sharing authority)
4. **Fresh Content**: Blog posts keep site updated for Google
5. **Social Signals**: OG tags help with social sharing

### The Results
When someone searches for any of your keywords, Google will:
- Find multiple pages about your event
- See them linking to each other
- See fresh blog content
- Rank them at the top
