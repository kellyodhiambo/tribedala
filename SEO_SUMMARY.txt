  1. Write great content (SEO can't fix bad content)
  2. Use descriptive titles (50-60 chars)
  3. Write good descriptions (150-160 chars)
  4. Add alt text to images
  5. Link to related content
  6. Update old content regularly
  7. Monitor search console weekly
